A mobile app for cyclists (React Native/Expo), with route planning, contextual weather, and community reporting. Organized by feature domains (rides, weather, reports, favorites) with Supabase as the backend. Not yet published to app stores; the repository has 27 commits, a Jest test suite, and a continuous integration pipeline.
